About Me

Hi, I’m Kenta Utsunomiya. I’m a Software Engineer at Google Ireland.

I have a deep-rooted passion for coding and have been immersed in technology since I was eight years old.

My background is heavily focused on mathematical thinking. During my undergraduate years, I researched complex networks, and I later earned my Master’s degree with a thesis centered on mathematical physics.

In today’s AI-driven era, there is a growing sentiment that "delivering value" is more important than the actual craft of coding. This shift has unfortunately led to a decline in people pursuing software engineering as a core discipline. However, I believe that in times like these, "old-school" software engineers like myself play a crucial role—especially when it comes to rigorous code reviews and mentoring the next generation of engineers.

I am not currently looking for a new role. However, if you would like to share a job description, please note that I am only considering positions based in Japan (ideally Tokyo or Fukuoka).

As a Japanese citizen, I do not require a work permit to work in Japan. While I currently hold an Irish work permit (Stamp 4), I am unfortunately not accepting any opportunities based in Dublin at this time. Thank you for your understanding.
